Dataset: IBM Telco Customer Churn Dataset

Public source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn

Included data file:
Code/WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv

Target column:
Churn

Task type:
Binary classification, where Churn = Yes is encoded as 1 and Churn = No is encoded as 0.

Brief description:
The dataset contains customer demographic attributes, telecom service subscriptions,
contract information, billing/payment variables, tenure, monthly charges, total charges,
and whether the customer churned.
